About me

I’m an FX artist. I’ll graduate from BYU in April 2027 with a bachelor’s degree in computer science with an emphasis in animation. I specialize in Houdini simulations, using Pyro, FLIP, MPM, Vellum, and other solvers based on what each shot needs.

I contributed FX to seven shots in the award-winning short Honey Business and currently build FX for Sandwich Kwon Do. I also composite in Nuke and texture in Substance Painter. I’m continuing to deepen my Houdini skills and broaden the range of effects I can create.
